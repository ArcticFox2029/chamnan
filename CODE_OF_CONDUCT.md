# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be straight with people, and be kind about it. Say what you measured, say what you are
unsure of, and take a correction the way you would want yours taken.

## What this project expects

chamnan is a small tool with a large amount of written-down reasoning in it. Most of what
happens here is one person telling another that a number is wrong, a claim is unsupported,
or an approach will not hold. That work only stays useful if it is possible to be wrong out
loud without it costing anything.

So:

- **Argue with the claim, never with the person.** "That measurement averages two populations"
  is the whole review. "You do not know what you are doing" is not a review at all.
- **Bring the number or the command.** A disagreement backed by something someone else can
  run ends quickly. One backed by confidence does not end.
- **Say when you were wrong, briefly, and move on.** This repository's own history is full of
  corrections to figures it had already published. That is the standard, not an embarrassment.
- **Assume the other person read less than you did, not that they cared less.** Missing context
  is the usual explanation and it is cheap to fix.
- **English is most contributors' second language.** Read past the phrasing to the point.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, demeaning comments about who somebody is rather than what they
wrote, unwelcome attention of any kind, publishing someone's private information, and
sustained disruption of a discussion after being asked to stop.

## Scope

Issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages and code review in this repository, and
any space where someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

Email the maintainer at **46153841+ArcticFox2029@users.noreply.github.com**, or open a private
security advisory if the matter is sensitive. Reports are read by the maintainer only. You
will get a reply; you will not get a lecture about whether you should have been bothered.

If the maintainer is the problem, say so publicly in an issue. A project this size has no
committee to escalate to, and pretending otherwise would be worse than admitting it.

## Enforcement

In order: a private word, a public correction, a temporary block, a permanent one. Which step
a thing starts at depends on how much harm it did, not on how it was phrased.
